## Huong dan dat ten

Muc tieu: ten ngan, ro nghia, de lap lai trong truyen, khong pho truong.

### Nguyen tac
- Ngan va truc quan: 2-4 tu, toi da 6 tu.
- Dung tong giong voi the loai: co phong, hien dai, khoa hoc vien tuong, do thi.
- Mot he phan cap nhat quan: neu co cap bac, giu cung mot bo tu vung.
- Uu tien ten co the dung truc tiep trong van ban.

### Mau nhanh
- Dia diem: [dac diem] + [loai dia diem]
- To chuc: [ly tuong/quyen luc] + [hinh thuc]
- Vat pham: [vat lieu/tac dung] + [vat the]
- Khai niem: [quy tac] + [pham vi]

### Tranh
- Qua nhieu tu hoa my rong nghia.
- Ten giong nhau giua nhan vat, vat pham, dia danh.
- Dung dau hai cham hang loat trong tieu de chuong.
